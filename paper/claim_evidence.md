# Manuscript claim and evidence map

This file follows the paper's scientific scope. Algebra conditional on the
closure is distinguished from evidence that the closure describes networks.

| Claim | Support | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Balance generates heterogeneous operating points and conditional local moments. | Balanced EI one-site self-consistency, closed positive-erf moments, measured local-law comparisons. | Conditional Gaussian and population-kernel concentration are asymptotic assumptions, not exact finite-network facts. |
| Population covariance geometry reduces to a small response resolvent. | Full conditional cavity algebra, diagonal restoration, population energy accounting, scalar and directed-lag checks. | This does not prove nonlinear cavity decoupling or a full covariance spectrum. |
| Premature spectral or gain averaging raises predicted total and population PR. | Population nonnegative-series proof for common leak, nonnegative response matrix, radius below one; independently reproduced numerical controls. | Fixed original local law and numerator; not a comparison between arbitrary self-consistent interventions. |
| Population dimensions and temporal alignment determine joint PR through an exact variance-weighted identity. | Finite-record trace algebra; temporal Gram overlap is linear CKA, with explicit primary credit. Parameter-only pair matrices predict overlap. | The identity and measure themselves are not claimed as new. Cross-window ratio estimates have finite-record error; temporal alignment is not a communication dimension. |
| Fraction and degree controls operate on different pieces of the theory. | Fixed-W/fixed-J0F composition identity; exact finite-pool degree identity and recomputed conditional law. | Each control path must state held quantities; finite-count row correlations and coherent stability need checks. |
| Correcting within-window centering matters for PR. | Exact independent-window estimator identity; full16 historical duration campaign; 1/B diagnostic and block/gap checks. | Corrected ratio is not unbiased; actual windows require mixing and duration checks. |
| Nonuniform degree intervention produces a large dimension change with different E/I local laws. | All 24 original degree networks complete, including the staged six-network extension. Endpoint mean dimensions differ by 8.41-fold at N4000 against 10.34-fold predicted; local moments and population energies are retained. | Largest-size endpoint PR errors are −6.7% and +14.8%. The N2000 excess of 30.3% is retained, so this is not a uniform quantitative validation. Count rounding and tested recording changes do not repair all gaps. |
| The original factor-two fraction discrepancy does not persist in the tested larger-network ensemble PR. | All 40 original composition/size records complete. At60:40, mean d is 0.029417 at N2000 and 0.029973 at N4000 against 0.029894 predicted; both pointwise 95% mean-error intervals lie within ±10%. N2000 nu/energy also agree with separate ingredient intervals reported. | This supports a finite-record ensemble comparison regime at the tested parameters, not a universal N1000 correction or accurate individual-network prediction. N4000energy and gain residuals remain visible; ingredient convergence is not uniformly monotone. |
| The degree and composition sweeps test the curves between the original settings. | The 81-network follow-up adds 12 degree and nine composition settings at N = 2000 and three selected settings on each axis at N = 4000, with three networks per cell. [Seed-level data and paired contrasts](../experiments/dense_sweeps/summary.json) | Pointwise uncertainty uses independent networks within each cell. The 4000-neuron results cover the selected settings only. |

The scalar critical crossover is supporting analytical material in the archive.
It is not a prerequisite for the focused EI paper and is not attributed
uniquely to heterogeneity: bias also produces local curvature.
