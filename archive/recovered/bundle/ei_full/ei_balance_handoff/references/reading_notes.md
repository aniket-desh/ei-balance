# Relevant primary sources

These notes record which ideas enter this project. They are not a systematic literature review. Metadata were checked on 22 October 2026. The verified bibliography is in `manuscript/references.bib`.

- [Clark, Abbott, and Litwin-Kumar, 2023](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.131.118401): the two-site construction, directed interactions, and scalar dimension benchmark. The calculation must recover its homogeneous limit. The cubic onset law is a reference result, not claimed as new here.
- [Clark, Marschall, van Meegen, and Litwin-Kumar, 2025](https://doi.org/10.1103/2jt7-c8cq): Appendix J already uses averages of same-neuron spectral and response products for general heterogeneous nonlinearities. This narrows the novelty claim. Our target is the operating-point distribution generated by balance, its conditioning on incoming connectivity, and the resulting critical geometry. Random-mode structure and task learning are not added to the model.
- [Clark, June 2026 version](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.23489v3): local response and nonlinear residual provide the fixed-connectivity representation. The preprint concerns a fixed weight realization in a large-network expansion. It is not a theory at fixed finite in-degree. Its distinction between Gaussian cavity fields and non-Gaussian full-network corrections is retained.
- [Clark, October 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.12426v1): the full Lyapunov spectrum is separate from activity participation ratio. We use that distinction and a maximum-exponent check. We do not claim to extend the full forward-backward construction to EI networks.
- [Clark and Beiran, 2025](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2404039122): structured inter-region currents motivate keeping coherent population feedback explicit. Its routing and communication-subspace results are outside the present claims.
- [Kadmon and Sompolinsky, 2015](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevX.5.041030): positive-rate, sign-constrained EI networks, Gaussian high in-degree approximation, static heterogeneity, and the two-replica stability operator form the one-site foundation.
- [Wakhloo, June 2026 version](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.24141v2): fixed-connectivity diagrammatics offer a route to higher cross-site moments. A full EI spectral density remains outside the completed pair calculation.

The author's [publication list](https://dclark.io/) was checked for recent work. The task-trained circuit preprint, task-selection paper, and continuous-attractor work address learning, routing, or designed symmetry. Those mechanisms are not needed for the independent-disorder balanced model and were not added merely to broaden the project.

The closed critical coefficient uses the [digamma integral, DLMF 5.9.15](https://dlmf.nist.gov/5.9#E15). The HTML reference was inspected; the TeX endpoint was unavailable.

No priority claim for the three-halves EI participation-ratio law is made on the basis of this targeted search alone. Its derivation and numerical verification here are independent of a priority claim.
