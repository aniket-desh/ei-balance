# Final independent review

The completed scoped round passes the mathematical accounting and claim
checks below. This review does not certify the nonlinear cavity assumptions
or claim a uniform finite-network error bound.

| Item | Review result |
|---|---|
| Conditional one-site and two-site algebra | Checked, including source averaging order, population resolvent, diagonal restoration, Fourier orientation, PR normalizations, and fixed-law averaging inequalities. |
| Coherent and endogenous-label qualifications | Explicit. Gaussian row regression requires an independent fixed reservoir mean vector; low rank or Hurwitz mean connectivity alone is insufficient. |
| Degree-row corrections | Exact finite-subset covariance, pair contraction, quadratic variance, and third-cumulant identities checked. Exhaustive quadratic-variance enumeration agrees within 9e-16. |
| Primary campaign | All 40 composition and 24 degree networks complete, with staged allocation and every outcome retained. The primary estimator remains T8000/B500/gap0. |
| Record accounting | All 72 run records pass control-path and normalization checks: 64 primary, five step comparisons, and three outlier follow-ups. Maximum population-symmetry error is below 9e-17; the joint-population PR identity agrees within 3e-17. |
| Composition inference | N2000 means and their six pointwise intervals are correctly described as finite-record ensemble comparisons. No predeclared 15% equivalence test, simultaneous 95% bound, individual-network guarantee, or monotone ingredient convergence is claimed. |
| Degree inference | The large degree effect transfers to nonuniform E/I local laws. Quantitative residuals remain, including N4000 PR errors -6.7% and +14.8%; they remain visible in the paper. |
| Population geometry | Temporal Gram alignment/linear CKA and the variance-weighted identity relating joint and population PR are exact. Empirical normalized geometry is secondary and its ratio-estimation limitations are stated. |
| Auxiliary numerical evidence | Four selected step comparisons have PR changes below 1.1%; an atypical N1000 network remains sensitive. Its same-step backend and three-level T16000 comparisons do not establish a converged invariant covariance. Four selected N2000 tangent checks are positive, without classifying all primary networks. |
| Novelty | Existing same-label moments and general heterogeneity machinery are credited. The work is presented as a conditional balanced EI specialization, control-path analysis, and validation, without a broad priority claim. |

The final main-text interpretation is appropriately limited: the original
factor-two 60:40 discrepancy does not persist in the tested larger-network
ensemble mean PR, while N1000 remains a prediction limitation with unresolved
physical, recording, and numerical contributions. The formal first-order
correction is not claimed to repair it. Measured-statistic substitutions,
the partial frozen-source correction, and its unsuccessful assumptions remain
separate from parameter-only predictions.
