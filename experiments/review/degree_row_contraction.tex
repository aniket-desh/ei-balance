\section{Degree constraints and the finite-reservoir pair source}
\label{sec:review-degree-contraction}

This audit checks whether replacing independent Bernoulli edges by an
exchangeable degree ensemble changes the leading pair source. The answer
requires a condition on coherent population projections, not just the
single-edge variance. The calculation is exact for probe rows independent
of a specified reservoir covariance. Applying it to the original recurrent
network retains the usual cavity-independence assumption.

\subsection{The exact row covariance}
Let a presynaptic pool contain $M$ neurons, and let $A_j\in\{0,1\}$ be the
indicators in one exchangeable row. Uniformly sample a subset after drawing
its size $K$. Suppose
\[
 \mathbb E K=Mp,\qquad
 \operatorname{Var}K=\chi_{\rm eff}Mp(1-p).
\]
The effective dispersion is the variance actually generated, including
stochastic rounding. Every edge has mean $p$ and variance $p(1-p)$.
Writing $K=\sum_jA_j$ gives
\begin{align*}
 \operatorname{Var}K
 &=Mp(1-p)+M(M-1)\operatorname{Cov}(A_j,A_k),\qquad j\ne k,\\
 \operatorname{Cov}(A_j,A_k)
 &=\frac{(\chi_{\rm eff}-1)p(1-p)}{M-1}.
\end{align*}
If the centered coupling is $w(A_j-p)/\sqrt N$ and
$G=w^2p(1-p)$, its row covariance is
\[
 S=\frac GN\left[
   \frac{M-\chi_{\rm eff}}{M-1}I_M+
   \frac{\chi_{\rm eff}-1}{M-1}\mathbf1\mathbf1^\top\right].
\]
With $u=\mathbf1/\sqrt M$ and $P=uu^\top$, this is
\[
 S=\frac GN\left[a I_M+bP\right],\quad
 a=\frac{M-\chi_{\rm eff}}{M-1},\quad
 b=\frac{M(\chi_{\rm eff}-1)}{M-1}.
\]
Its eigenvalue on the constant vector is $G\chi_{\rm eff}/N$; its
$M-1$ orthogonal eigenvalues are $Ga/N$. For exactly fixed counts,
$S=(G/N)[M/(M-1)](I-P)$: population-constant fluctuations are removed
exactly. Different receiving rows remain independent in this ensemble.

\subsection{The exact pair-source contraction}
Let $C$ denote any real reservoir cross-covariance matrix at a fixed lag.
Take two independent, centered probe rows $j_i,j_k$ with row covariances
$S_a,S_c$, independent of $C$. Their shared-reservoir cross-input is
$Y=j_i C j_k^\top$. Conditioning on $C$,
\begin{align*}
 \mathbb E[Y^2\mid C]
 &=\sum_{j,l,m,n}C_{jl}C_{mn}
    \mathbb E[j_{ij}j_{im}]\mathbb E[j_{kl}j_{kn}]\\
 &=\sum_{j,l,m,n}C_{jl}C_{mn}(S_a)_{jm}(S_c)_{ln}\\
 &=\boxed{\operatorname{tr}(S_a C S_c C^\top)}.
\end{align*}
No fourth-cumulant assumption enters this bilinear, two-independent-row
identity. It does not describe a repeated probe row.

Let $I_b$ project onto presynaptic population $b$, $u_b$ be its normalized
constant vector, and $P_b=u_bu_b^\top$. For receiving class $a$ write
\[
 S_a=\sum_b(s_{ab}I_b+t_{ab}P_b),\quad
 s_{ab}=\frac{G_{ab}}N\frac{M_b-\chi_{ab}}{M_b-1},\quad
 t_{ab}=\frac{G_{ab}}N\frac{M_b(\chi_{ab}-1)}{M_b-1}.
\]
Expanding the trace yields
\begin{align*}
 \mathbb E[Y^2\mid C]
 =\sum_{b,d}\big[&s_{ab}s_{cd}\|I_bCI_d\|_F^2\\
 &+t_{ab}s_{cd}\|I_d C^\top u_b\|^2\\
 &+s_{ab}t_{cd}\|I_b C u_d\|^2\\
 &+t_{ab}t_{cd}|u_b^\top C u_d|^2\big].
\end{align*}
The first term tends to the independent-edge contraction. The other terms
are explicit coherent-projection corrections. For equal-time $C$, symmetry
relates the left and right projections; unequal-lag covariance requires
both. Diagonal factors $s_{ab}$ also differ from $G_{ab}/N$ by relative
$O(M_b^{-1})$ for bounded dispersion.

\subsection{When the leading pair equation survives}
Suppose the number of populations is fixed, all fractions are nonzero,
$G$ and $\chi$ stay bounded, and $\|C\|_F^2=O(N)$. The independent-edge
source is then $O(N^{-1})$. The coherent terms are $O(N^{-2})$ if
\[
 \|Cu_b\|^2+\|C^\top u_b\|^2=O(1)
 \quad\hbox{for every population }b.
\]
Under these conditions degree constraints do not change the leading pair
equation after the one-site law has been recomputed. A bounded operator norm
is sufficient for the projection condition, but is stronger than necessary.
Stable mean connectivity by itself is not a proof of this condition in a
nonlinear network. An unusually amplified coherent mode can invalidate it.

For one population and exactly fixed counts the ratio to the Bernoulli
source is particularly transparent:
\[
 \frac{\mathbb E[Y^2\mid C]_{\chi=0}}
      {(G/N)^2\|C\|_F^2}
 =\left(\frac{N}{N-1}\right)^2
  \left[1-
  \frac{\|Cu\|^2+\|C^\top u\|^2-|u^\top Cu|^2}{\|C\|_F^2}\right].
\]
Thus an extensive participation ratio alone does not guarantee negligible
correction: a single coherent eigenvalue of order $\sqrt N$ can contribute
$O(N)$ covariance energy while contributing only $O(\sqrt N)$ to the trace.
The diagnostic is the projection fraction, not just $D/N$.

\subsection{What this does and does not repair}
The formulas give a parameter-free finite-reservoir input-source diagnostic
once $C$ is measured. They are not a closed stationary finite-network
correction: recurrent dependence of probe rows, local responses, source
changes, and response dressing must be treated consistently. In particular,
they cannot be inserted into the final PR denominator as a fitted amplitude.
For the nonuniform degree experiment we record population mean time courses
and $\|Cu_b\|^2$, alongside the population covariance energies. Small measured
projection fractions support this specific step of the degree extension;
large fractions identify a concrete failure of its leading assumptions.
