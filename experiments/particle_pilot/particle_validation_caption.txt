Finite-count particle mean field compared with held-out E/I networks. All physical networks have 2,000 cells, 80% E and target population rates of .3. (a) The 10th, 25th, 50th, 75th and 90th percentiles of each cell's full-record mean input at χ = 0, after subtracting the corresponding Gaussian offset. Blue circles and red squares are three held-out E and I populations, respectively. Blue solid and red dashed lines show the mean prediction across three pools of 400 particles per type. The Gaussian prediction lies on zero. (b) Joint population PR divided by N. Filled circles are independent networks with the four-window estimate from a duration of 8,000 and 500-unit windows. Open diamonds are the three 400-particle solves at each dispersion. Dots and diamonds are displaced horizontally for visibility. The dotted line connects the Gaussian predictions. (c) Out-of-half RMSE in predicting single-cell mean gain with measured cell-specific temporal variance divided by RMSE using the measured population-average variance. Each prediction uses the cell's mean input and variance estimate from the opposite half of the recording. Small points show individual networks and line segments connect their means, with the same E/I colors as (a). A ratio below one favors cell-specific variance. Panel (c) is a diagnostic using held-out measurements. The theoretical predictions in (a,b) use only network parameters.
