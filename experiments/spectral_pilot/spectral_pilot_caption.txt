Fixed-point eigenmodes and their measured equal-time input variance in four independently constructed Bernoulli E/I networks with 80% excitatory cells. (a) N = 250, seed 7101. (b) N = 250, seed 7102. (c) N = 500, seed 7101. (d) N = 500, seed 7102. Each dot is an eigenvalue of J diag φ′(x*) at the first verified fixed point found for that draw. Both members of a complex-conjugate pair are shown. The shared color scale shows u† Cₓ u for the corresponding unit-norm right eigenvector u, with square-root spacing for visibility. Cₓ is the centered covariance of the simulated input over 2,000 time units after a 500-unit burn-in. The leak places the linear stability boundary at Re λ = 1 (dashed). In (b) the input reached a stable fixed point before recording. Its temporal variance is zero at the stored precision. The other three recordings fluctuated, and each gave a positive association between Re λ and projected variance in both separately centered halves. Two networks also admitted alternate verified roots, changing the magnitude of the association.
