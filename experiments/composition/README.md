# Fixed-input composition validation

All forty prospective composition jobs are complete. Start with
[`resolution.md`](resolution.md) for the scoped scientific conclusion and
[`prospective_findings.md`](prospective_findings.md) for the full numerical
tables. The leading theory has an empirically supported domain; a controlled
parameter-only repair of every N1000 network is not claimed.

The central question is whether the 60:40 discrepancy is a reproducible error
of the conditional EI prediction, and where it enters. The protocol keeps
`G F` and `J0 F` fixed and uses fresh networks with a common estimator.

- `prospective_protocol_proposal.json` records the proposal made before new
  outcomes. The runtime campaign owns the exact accepted allocation and all
  simulation results; recommended sizes are not counts of completed runs.
- `composition_theory_targets.json` contains parameter-only leading predictions
  and separately labeled formal first-order values at N1000/2000/4000.
- `composition_diagnosis.tex` gives normalizations, exact error accounting,
  the strict finite-size ratio expansion, source/gain substitutions, and an
  exact population-coherent projection of covariance energy.
- `historical_discrepancy_accounting.json` records the existing long-record
  cohort. Both ingredients contribute to its lower ratio of ensemble means.
- `historical_hybrid_audit.json` preserves the prior held-out substitutions.
  Measured one-site laws repair one outlier but do not supply a general repair.
- `geometry_projection.py` and `projection_identity_check.json` verify the
  projection identity against explicit projectors on small positive matrices.

No new finite-network correction is claimed here. The first-order stationary
closure shifts the 60:40 PR upward and cannot explain the historical negative
gap. Common-window prospective results, including uncertainty and every
outcome, determine the supported validity claim. Four seeds per condition
can discriminate a factor-two failure much more readily than a 10–15% bias.

## Added targeted diagnostics

`size_series_analysis_plan.json` fixes the estimator, paired-seed contrasts,
and accuracy reporting before this analyst inspected the N2000/N4000 group
outcomes. The extension cohort remains distinguishable from the initial pilot.
`aggregate_prospective.py` keeps every planned status and separately reports
raw moments, mean network PR, ratio of mean moments, population energy,
normalized geometry, local moments, and the static/dynamic variance partition.

`selected_local_addon.py` analyzes only the prespecified retained seed3001
trajectories. It saves compact per-neuron block moments and checks across-block
gain moments and input variances. Its Gaussianized substitutions use measured
local moments and are diagnostic calibrations, not independent predictions.
It also reconstructs PR exactly as uncorrelated dimension divided by the
correlation-energy penalty, using the same four-window matching as the primary
estimator. Raw trajectories remain outside the deliverable archive.

`static_source_diagnosis.py` regenerates the exact connectivity and decomposes
static disorder at the measured rate means into diagonal-source, cross-source,
and centering pieces. The mean-state equation is checked separately to expose
finite-record or discretization residuals. These identities locate a residual;
they do not predict a deterministic finite-size correction.

`static_partition_susceptibility.py` verifies the Gaussian functional derivative
by independent finite differences, computes its fixed-total-variance response,
and measures the full DMFT degree tangent at chi1 with step halving. The
susceptibility is deliberately distinguished from a complete quenched theory.
See `PAPER_USE.md` for the intended main-text, appendix, and limitation roles.

The final local calibration and static-source summaries each cover 28 networks:
the four selected initial records plus all 24 composition extensions.
`local_calibration_summary.json` retains the population-Gaussian, empirical
static-label/common-variance, and individual-label/individual-variance gain
levels separately. `static_source_summary.json` keeps sample means and
connectivity-to-connectivity variation separate. `gain_order_diagnostic.json`
checks existing formal coefficients without fitting a second-order repair.
The diagnostic watcher stopped normally after all forty jobs completed.
