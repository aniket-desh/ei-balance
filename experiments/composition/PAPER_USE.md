# How the composition work belongs in the paper

The main result is the conditional EI construction and its population geometry.
The composition campaign tests its accuracy on a fixed-aggregate-input path.
No new finite-size expansion is adopted as a completed correction.

| Material | Manuscript role | Claim it can support |
|---|---|---|
| Parameter-only leading curves and prospective nu/E/d/block-energy measurements | Main results | Quantitative error and observed size dependence under the stated protocol. |
| Static versus temporal variance and selected local conditional-law checks | Main mechanism or supporting panel | Finite networks can redistribute variance between operating points and temporal activity; a total marginal variance check alone is insufficient. |
| Historical full16 duration/centering result | Main validation with compact explanation | The original two-network factor-two discrepancy is not representative of the expanded cohort, and the old estimator had an identifiable bias; a residual remains at N1000. |
| Exact ratio-of-means versus mean-ratio identity | Methods/appendix, with both reported | PR agreement can hide correlated ingredient errors. |
| Strict first-order expansion and its wrong-direction historical shift | Appendix plus material limitation | The formal retained correction does not repair the original finite-network comparison. |
| Frozen-total-variance partition Jacobian and degree tangent | Appendix or short mechanism explanation | An explicit conditional functional has a sensitive partition direction; this is not a full quenched susceptibility. |
| Fixed-network static source identity | Appendix diagnostic | Separates empirical presynaptic mean-rate distributions, squared-coupling weighting, and remaining cross-source terms without fitting PR. |
| Population-uniform projection sector | Appendix/control | Tests this specified coherent subspace; a small value does not rule out nonuniform collective modes. |
| Prior measured-local-law hybrid substitutions | Diagnostic appendix and limitations | Individual-rate corrections alone do not uniformly repair pair energy. |
| Full finite-network response/gain correction or complete disorder law | Open limitation | No closed predictive repair has been established by these diagnostics. |

Conventional network mean intervals describe sampling uncertainty in the
reported finite-record observable. They are not uniform approximation bounds,
and including the theory in a broad interval is not an equivalence result.
The exact largest-size data and their precision determine the supported domain.
