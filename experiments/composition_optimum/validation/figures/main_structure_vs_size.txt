Fitted coefficients of the composition structure divided by their parameter-only values, against network size. T_abc (block ab from source population c; filled circles: E sources, open squares: I sources; color gives the block: EE blue, EI teal, II red) are fitted by weighted least squares to the cell means of the block energies over ten fractions; v_E and v_I (diamonds) are the means of the per-population rate variances. Bars: 95% stratified-bootstrap intervals; shaded band: +/-10%. Small T_abc (T_EEI, T_EII, T_III) are poorly determined at the high fractions used. Cohort: composition path at the reference couplings, Bernoulli edges, 8,000 recorded leak times, primary estimator window (four-window mean difference, window 500, no gap); 64 networks per cell at N = 2,000, 16 at 4,000 and 8,000, 4 at 16,000 (reference rates only); every network independently generated.
