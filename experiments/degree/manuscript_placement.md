# Where the degree work belongs in the paper

The main text needs the nonuniform intervention and what it establishes:
the static source depends on both the distribution of neuronal mean rates
and the degree-count dispersion; the one-site laws feed the same population
covariance-energy construction; the observed dimensionality falls strongly
when dispersion increases. State the actual external-input control path.
Keep the population energy normalization and intermediate local-law checks
visible so a favorable PR ratio cannot conceal ingredient errors.

The initial18 test also supplies a material limitation. Its N2000 endpoints
have mean PR errors of -10% and +30%; small changes of block gaps and record
duration do not explain those discrepancies. The subsequent N4000 extension
is declared as a staged follow-up and cannot erase the earlier observations.

The appendix should contain the exact finite-pool count/covariance algebra,
the O(1/M) static variance factor, the third-cumulant enumeration, numerical
quadrature refinement, the away-from-onset static susceptibility, and the
cross-window identities used to measure local moments. Those calculations
make the control and measurements reproducible without interrupting the
main conditional-construction argument.

The measured-statistic local-variance feedback calculation is a diagnostic
appendix result. It shows that nonuniform temporal variances and their
covariance with input means contribute to finite-network rate heterogeneity,
which can then account for static input spread. It does not predict those
temporal-variance fluctuations from the microscopic model or repair PR
without measurements. Its status must stay explicit even when the measured
static-source substitution agrees closely.

The limitations section should distinguish:

- The leading degree closure needs a self-averaging asynchronous conditional
  state and the weighted-response/projection conditions from the cavity audit.
- The finite-N degree ensemble has joint edge cumulants beyond its marginal
  variance; Bernoulli correction coefficients do not transfer by replacing
  a single chi parameter.
- Local Gaussian laws and concentrating temporal kernels have observable
  finite-size errors in these cohorts.
- Correcting covariance-energy bias does not make the ratio unbiased, prove
  temporal independence, or establish chaotic branch membership.
- Selected or captured local-moment diagnostics cover the stated subset,
  while the primary geometry comparison retains every network.

The remaining full finite-size theory and attractor probability problem are
not required extensions of this degree test. The main claims should follow
the completed comparison rather than treating those extensions as automatic
next tasks.
