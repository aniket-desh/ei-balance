\section{An exact finite-pool third-cumulant check}
\label{sec:degree-third-cumulant}

Changing degree dispersion preserves marginal edge variance but changes
joint moments among edges in a receiving row. This matters if one attempts
to reuse a finite-size Bernoulli correction for a different degree ensemble.
The leading static-variance modification alone does not specify that
correction.

For \(M>2\), let
\[
 p_2=\frac{E[K(K-1)]}{M(M-1)},\qquad
 p_3=\frac{E[K(K-1)(K-2)]}{M(M-1)(M-2)}.
\]
Exchangeability and \(E A_j=p\) give the three possible central third moments
\begin{align*}
 \kappa_{111}&=E(A_j-p)^3=p(1-p)(1-2p),\\
 \kappa_{112}&=E[(A_j-p)^2(A_k-p)]
            =(1-2p)(p_2-p^2),\qquad j\ne k,\\
 \kappa_{123}&=E[(A_j-p)(A_k-p)(A_l-p)]
            =p_3-3pp_2+2p^3,\qquad j,k,l\ {\rm distinct}.
\end{align*}
The second equality uses
\((A_j-p)^2=(1-2p)A_j+p^2\), valid for binary \(A_j\).

For fixed numbers \(w_j\), write \(S_n=\sum_jw_j^n\). Expanding the cube of
\(\sum_j(A_j-p)w_j\), grouping repeated indices, and using
\[
 \sum_{j\ne k}w_j^2w_k=S_1S_2-S_3,\qquad
 \sum_{\substack{j,k,l\\{\rm all\ distinct}}}w_jw_kw_l
 =S_1^3-3S_1S_2+2S_3
\]
gives the exact formula
\[
 E\left[\left(\sum_j(A_j-p)w_j\right)^3\right]
 =(\kappa_{111}-3\kappa_{112}+2\kappa_{123})S_3
  +3(\kappa_{112}-\kappa_{123})S_1S_2
  +\kappa_{123}S_1^3.
\]
Multiplying by \(t_{ab}^3/N^{3/2}\) gives the corresponding synaptic-input
cumulant. For independent Bernoulli edges,
\(\kappa_{112}=\kappa_{123}=0\). For an exactly fixed integer count \(K=Mp\),
\[
 \kappa_{112}=-\frac{\kappa_{111}}{M-1},\qquad
 \kappa_{123}=\frac{2\kappa_{111}}{(M-1)(M-2)}.
\]
For a centered source \(S_1=0\), this reduces to
\[
 E\left[\left(\sum_j(A_j-p)w_j\right)^3\right]
 =\kappa_{111}\frac{M^2}{(M-1)(M-2)}S_3.
\]
Thus the leading cubic cumulant of a centered heterogeneous source can
survive even at exactly fixed degree. Conversely a constant source gives
\(\sum_j(A_j-p)w_j=w(K-Mp)=0\) identically at that endpoint; the three index
sectors cancel exactly. Dropping the distinct-index cumulants would miss
this cancellation.

Exact enumeration of all \(2^8\) subsets at six degree dispersions, together
with an integer-fixed-count example, verifies the identities for constant,
noncentered, and centered asymmetric sources to numerical precision. This
is a finite-pool moment audit, not a nonlinear stationary correction. In
particular, no parameter-only prediction for the finite-network temporal
variance distribution or its associated PR shift follows from these
identities alone.
