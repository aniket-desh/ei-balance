At fixed 80:20 E:I, the panels show (a) joint dimension per network neuron, (b) across-neuron variance of finite-record input means, (c) temporal input variance and (d) E–E, twice E–I, and I–I energy per network neuron. Lines are parameter-only predictions. The square-root horizontal scale resolves the low degree dispersions. Circles, squares and triangles identify N = 1,000, 2,000 and 4,000. Half-filled markers summarize the 24 original networks and filled markers summarize 45 new networks. Each cell, size and cohort has its own mean and one across-network SE. Faint dots in (a) show individual networks. Hollow points at χ=1 are the separate Bernoulli composition references (n=12 at N=2,000 and n=4 at N=4,000). The original cohort and the extension remain separate when they share a setting. The new grid comprises 12 dispersions at N=2,000 and three selected dispersions at N=4,000, with three realizations per size and dispersion.

The network runs used mean rate targets 0.3, identical nominal edge marginals, a fixed leading balance drive and a theory-derived order-one offset for each χ. The primary record is T=8,000 with a 500-time burn-in, integration step 0.05, sampling step 0.25, and a 500-time four-window estimator at zero minimum gap. The raw input means in (b) include finite-time mean-estimation noise.
