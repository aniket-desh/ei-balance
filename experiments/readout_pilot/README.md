# Fixed-size E and I readouts

This pilot measures the participation ratio of the temporal rate covariance among 100 uniformly sampled E neurons or 100 uniformly sampled I neurons. Each of four connectivity seeds supplies one network at each excitatory fraction (0.80, 0.88 and 0.94), with 2,000 neurons and the same fixed-aggregate Bernoulli control as the dense campaign. Seeds 6101–6103 reuse its graph configurations. Seed 6104 adds one independent triplet. Its full covariance measurements stay in this pilot.

Across the four networks at each fraction, mean E readout PR was 28.63, 27.34 and 26.97 at the three fractions. Mean I readout PR was 30.37, 27.38 and 24.76. From 0.80 to 0.88 the paired E change was −1.29 cells (pointwise 95% interval −4.78 to 2.21) and the I change was −2.99 (−8.92 to 2.95). The pilot leaves both changes unresolved. Keeping only one of the eight prespecified readout labels across seeds gives E changes ranging from −4.36 to 1.77. The corresponding I changes range from −5.24 to −2.15. These ranges describe sensitivity to readout choice for the same four seed labels, with four networks at each fraction.

All twelve connectivity hashes match the corresponding full-network measurements. Seed 6101 reproduces the Mac dense recordings in the per-cell block moments of the sampled readout cells. The new 6104 readouts share the recordings used for their full-network statistics. The six readout jobs for seeds 6102–6103 share the graphs of the cloud measurements but follow different numerical trajectories on the Mac. At fraction 0.94 the inhibitory readouts overlap by 83.3% on average.

`protocol.json` fixes all neuron indices before activity measurement. For each seed, class and readout, `prepare.py` draws random priorities on class-relative neuron IDs with selection seed 20261119. The 100 smallest priorities among the cells present at each fraction give a uniform subset. Sharing priorities across fractions couples the subset choices. The eight readouts from each class can overlap. At 94% E there are only 120 I neurons to choose from.

`measure.py` forms the actual covariance block for each subset. For blocks $a,b$ with within-block covariances $C_a,C_b$ and mean-rate difference $u_{ab}$, it forms $D_{ab}=(C_a+C_b+u_{ab}u_{ab}^{T})/2$. The trace estimate averages $\operatorname{tr}(D_{ab})$ over the permitted pairs. The squared Frobenius norm averages $\operatorname{tr}(D_{ab}D_{cd})$ over all three matchings of every permitted four distinct windows. Their ratio, squared trace over energy, gives the raw PR in units of cells. The primary uses $T=8{,}000$, $B=500$ and a minimum gap of zero between selected blocks. Intervening blocks are allowed. Every network also retains the $T=4{,}000$ and one-empty-block checks.

`summary.json` takes the mean of eight measured PRs within each class and network before computing uncertainty across the four seed labels. Differences across fractions are paired by network seed. Pointwise 95% intervals use three degrees of freedom. Empirical variability across readouts is provided separately. The per-network files in `measurements/` retain every readout, its absolute cell IDs, block means, and the lower triangle of its within-block covariance matrices in NumPy `tril_indices` order.

Run `python3 plot_readouts.py` here to redraw the figure from the saved [summary](summary.json). For a fresh simulation, use a disposable checkout, move `measurements/` aside so the launcher sees the draws as pending, then run `python3 prepare.py`, `python3 run.py`, and `python3 summarize.py` from this directory. This regenerates the omitted block arrays under `measurements/`. The run releases temporary trajectories after atomically saving the sufficient block statistics and readout results. The summary compares the selected cells’ block means and variances with the original full-network capsules when available. Matching moments verify agreement on those statistics. A mismatch establishes that the recordings differ despite identical connectivity.
