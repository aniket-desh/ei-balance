# Independent replication of the fixed-point mode comparison

Specified before any of the following simulations were launched. The existing four-network pilot established the recording, continuation, and correlation methods. Its networks are excluded from every statistic below. The scientific unit is an independently generated network.

**Design.** Use 12 new 250-neuron networks (seeds 73101 through 73112) and 12 new 500-neuron networks (73201 through 73212). Use the pilot's Bernoulli graph, 80% E, target rates 0.3, population couplings and drive, midpoint step 0.05, burn-in 500, and recording of 2,000 leak times at spacing 0.25. Simulate all 24 draws regardless of states and effects. Local workers use one numerical thread each and the pilot's 1.5 GiB per-process watchdog. The source baseline is Git commit f6da876.

**Which fixed point.** Use the pilot's deterministic search order. Try the recording mean, first sample, and population offsets by direct solve. If all three fail, continue from the recording mean and then the population offsets. Accept full-strength roots with maximum absolute drift at most 1e-8. Distinct roots differ by at least 1e-5 in infinity norm. The first verified root is primary. Report every other verified root and failed attempt. Classify a recording as fluctuating if both independently centered half-records have mean temporal input variance greater than 1e-10. Retain quiet draws and root failures in the cohort counts. A quiet recording alone does not establish a stable fixed point.

**Primary comparison.** Within each fluctuating network with a verified root, rank unit right eigenvectors of the input coupling J diag φ′(x*) by real eigenvalue and by measured projected input variance. Use one member per conjugate pair and all real modes. Repeat for the centered first and second recording halves. Compare the full-record Spearman correlation to 64 permutations that shuffle each eigenvector's coordinates identically within E and within I. Use the same deterministic permutation stream for alternate roots within a network, and independent streams across networks. The main effect is observed correlation minus that network's median shuffled correlation. Report both sizes separately, pooled median effect and the exact one-sided sign test across independent eligible networks. Count ties separately. Compare the first verified root with all alternatives and retain each network's state and selection rule.

**Connection to rate covariance.** Take the right eigenvectors with largest real eigenvalues until at least 10% of real dimensions are included, completing each conjugate pair and any ties at the cutoff. Map them to rate tangent directions by diag φ′(x*), take the numerical real span, and orthonormalize. Compute the fractions of centered rate covariance trace and squared Frobenius energy contained in this subspace, and the participation ratio of the orthogonal complement relative to the full recording. Compare to 64 within-population permutations and 64 within-population and fixed-point-gain-decile permutations. Permute coordinates of the whole orthonormal basis together to preserve its dimension and internal geometry. Record full and split-half measurements and conditional nulls on the full record. These secondary comparisons assess the geometry of recorded activity in a subspace chosen from the fixed point. They use directly centered finite-record rate covariance. Report effects jointly with the primary comparison and include negative results. A linear projection of an observed recording is a geometric calculation.
