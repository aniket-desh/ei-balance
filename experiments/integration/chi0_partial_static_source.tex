\section{A partial fixed-degree source and why it is not a repair}
At nominal zero degree dispersion, the leading conditional law has
$\Delta_a=0$. Finite-network temporal variances can still vary across neurons.
Write their shifts as $\delta q_i$ and the mean-input shifts as $\delta\mu_i$.
For $m(\mu,q)=E_Y\phi(\mu+\sqrt qY)$, Gaussian differentiation gives
\begin{equation}
 \partial_\mu m=\alpha,\qquad
 \partial_q m=\tfrac12 E_Y\phi''(\mu+\sqrt qY)=\tfrac12\beta.
\end{equation}
Therefore
\begin{align}
 \delta m_i&=\alpha_a\delta\mu_i+\tfrac12\beta_a\delta q_i+\cdots,\\
 \operatorname{Var}_i m_i
 &=\alpha_a^2\Delta_a+\alpha_a\beta_a
     \operatorname{Cov}_i(\mu_i,q_i)
       +\tfrac14\beta_a^2\operatorname{Var}_i q_i+\cdots.
 \label{eq:partial-static-mixed}
\end{align}
Here the reference values are common within each population. For positive erf,
$\beta_a=-h_a\alpha_a/(1+q_a)$.

Let $S$ be the leak-filtered centered rate covariance of a frozen reservoir.
Let $\mathcal E^H_{bc}=N^{-1}\sum_{j\in b,k\in c}S_{jk}^2$, and $s_b=S_{jj}$
at the homogeneous leading law within population $b$. Under the independent
fixed-subset assumptions stated in the exact quadratic-variance note, a partial
leading source is
\begin{equation}
 V_{q,a}^{\rm frozen}=\frac2N\sum_{bc}G_{ab}G_{ac}
 [\mathcal E^H_{bc}-\delta_{bc}f_bs_b^2].
 \label{eq:partial-vq}
\end{equation}
The subtraction removes the diagonal covariance contribution whose incoming
weight sum is fixed by the count constraint. The filtered energy is computed
from the leading population resolvent, multiplying both frequency integrands
by $|H(\omega_1)|^2|H(\omega_2)|^2$ before integration.

If the mixed covariance in Eq.~\eqref{eq:partial-static-mixed} is discarded
and the independent static contraction $\Delta=W\operatorname{Var}(m)$ is
used, the algebra is
\begin{align}
 \Delta&=W\operatorname{diag}(\alpha^2)\Delta
       +W\operatorname{diag}(\beta^2/4)V_q^{\rm frozen},\\
 [I-W\operatorname{diag}(\alpha^2)]\Delta
       &=W\operatorname{diag}(\beta^2/4)V_q^{\rm frozen},\\
 \Delta^{\rm partial}
       &=[I-W\operatorname{diag}(\alpha^2)]^{-1}
         W\operatorname{diag}(\beta^2/4)V_q^{\rm frozen}.
\end{align}
No measured amplitude appears in this candidate. At $N=1000$ it gives
$V_q^{\rm frozen}=(.12190,.28683)$ and
$\Delta^{\rm partial}=(.02421,.03708)$ before rounding. In the selected
degree seed4001 case, cross-window measurements are approximately
$(.2076,.5473)$ and $(.0607,.0965)$, respectively. The nonzero measured mixed
covariances, approximately $(.01962,.05572)$, already contradict discarding
the middle term of Eq.~\eqref{eq:partial-static-mixed} at this order.

The independent frozen-row source also omits coherent noncentral terms and
endogenous source--row and probe-reinsertion effects. The calculation is
retained as an explicit partial source, with its assumptions and numerical
undershoot, and rejected as a complete stationary finite-size correction.
