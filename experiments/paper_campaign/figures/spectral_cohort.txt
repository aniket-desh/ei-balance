Fixed-point modes organize recorded fluctuations across network size and dispersion. (a) Eigenvalues of J diag(phi'(x*)) at the first verified fixed point of one network of 2,000 cells; color is the recorded input variance along each unit right eigenvector, and the dashed line is the instability threshold. (b-d) For each fluctuating Bernoulli network, the observed statistic (colored) beside its shuffle median (gray), joined by a line; horizontal bars are medians across networks. (b) Spearman correlation rho between a mode's real eigenvalue and its input variance, against within-population coordinate shuffles. (c) Fraction of squared rate-covariance energy inside the leading tenth of fixed-point directions, against gain-decile-matched shuffles. (d) PR of the recording after projecting out that subspace, relative to the full PR. (e) Fraction of networks that fluctuate and fraction with a verified fixed point. (f) Number of unstable fixed-point modes per neuron. (g,h) The comparisons of (b,c) at N = 1,000 for degree dispersion 0, 1 and 2. Networks: 32 at 250, 32 at 500, 32 at 1,000, 13 at 2,000 cells (Bernoulli); 16 each at dispersion 0 and 2.
