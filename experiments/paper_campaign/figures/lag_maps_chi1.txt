Two-lag pair moments at \chi=1, N = 2,000 (8 networks). psi_ab(tau1,tau2) is N times the mean over distinct pairs (i in a, j in b) of C_ij(tau1) C_ij(tau2). Top: parameter-only prediction. Middle: mean of cross-fitted network estimates, in which the two covariance factors come from disjoint halves of the recording so that estimation noise does not multiply itself. Each column shares one symmetric color scale; dashed contours mark negative values. Bottom: equal-lag slice (colored) and tau2 = 0 slice (gray) with one across-network standard error. No amplitude or timescale is fitted.
