The same-neuron source of covariance energy at Bernoulli dispersion, N = 2,000. Q_a(tau1,tau2) is the mean over neurons of the product of a neuron's own rate autocovariance at two lags, cross-fitted across recording halves. (a) Equal-lag slice and (b) tau2 = 0 slice, with predictions. (c) Predicted ratio of the source to the squared population-mean kernel; averaging spectra before taking the product replaces this ratio by one.
