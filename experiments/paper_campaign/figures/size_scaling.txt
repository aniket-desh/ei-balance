Finite-size residuals of the dimension and its two ingredients. Relative error of the across-network median against the large-N prediction, plotted against the inverse square root of network size (top axis: N). A leading finite-size correction would appear as a straight line through the origin. Bars are one across-network standard error. Networks per size: 48, 32, 24 (48 at 60:40), 12 and 6 for N = 500, 1,000, 2,000, 4,000 and 8,000, and 4 at N = 16,000 for dispersions 0, 1 and 2. Dots are networks; markers are across-network medians with a bootstrap standard error over fluctuating networks (medians, because at N = 500 a few weakly fluctuating networks have ill-conditioned ratios).
