Eigenvalue spectra of the whole-record rate covariance (ordinary centering, 8,000 leak times), normalized to unit mean and plotted against normalized rank; geometric mean over up to eight networks. (a) Degree dispersion at N = 2,000. (b) Excitatory fraction at N = 2,000. (c) Network size at dispersion 1, 80:20. These spectra include the finite-record noise floor and are shown for shape only; the manuscript's dimension estimates use the cross-window estimator.
