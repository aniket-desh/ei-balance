Seed-paired changes relative to 80% excitatory cells. Each marker is the mean over matched seeds of the difference between a network at the plotted fraction and the same-seed network at 0.80; bars are two-sided 95% Student-t intervals. Lines are the predicted differences. (a,b) 100-cell E and I readout PR. (c,d) Distinct-pair energies. (e) Total E dimension per network neuron. (f) Joint dimension per neuron. N = 2,000, Bernoulli edges, 48 seed-matched networks per fraction, 8,000 recorded leak times.
