Sensitivity to an empty window between estimator windows and to the integration step. (a) Relative change of D/N when the four estimator windows must be separated by one empty window. (b,c) Relative change of D/N and of the mean rate variance when the same graph and initial condition are integrated with a step of 0.025 or 0.125 instead of 0.05 (8,000 leak times each); a different step produces a different chaotic trajectory, so individual networks scatter. Bars are two-sided 95% t intervals over eight networks; dots are networks.
